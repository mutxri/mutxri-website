Most SMBs Are
Profitable on Paper —
But Cash Poor

  • Financial Statements Are Telling a Different Story : Your P&L says you're profitable, but your cash flow says otherwise. That gap is where profit is leaking. We find it.
  • No Real Visibility Into Margins : Without job costing and proper financial systems, you're making decisions on gut feel, not facts. You don't know which areas of your business are actually profitable.
